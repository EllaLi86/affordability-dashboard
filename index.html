EXPLAINABLE REVIEW ORDER

How priority is calculated

The 100-point score helps reviewers see the most time-sensitive applications first.

40

Financial gap

How far reported income falls below the household's modeled basic-needs threshold.

30

Housing instability

Documented homelessness, eviction risk, temporary housing, or severe rent burden.

18

Household needs

Children, very young children, single-caregiver households, and larger households.

12

Time waiting

One point for each completed week since submission, capped at twelve weeks.

Built-in guardrails

The score excludes name, language, geography, race, gender, disability, and other protected traits. It never auto-approves, denies, or determines eligibility. Staff can see every contributing factor and must record a reason for final decisions.
