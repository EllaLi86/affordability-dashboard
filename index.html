HUMAN-REVIEWED MATCHING

How matching and priority work

The dashboard keeps review priority separate from property eligibility.

01

Priority supports review order

Financial gap, housing instability, household needs, and time waiting can flag a case for earlier staff attention. The category never determines placement.

02

Properties have separate rules

Potential matches compare household income, household size, bedroom need, location preference, and published property requirements.

03

Missing evidence stays visible

A promising match is labeled “verification required” when documents needed by the property are missing.

04

Staff decide every next step

Staff review the source information, contact the applicant, confirm property rules, and submit applications.

AI assistance guardrails

Automation may summarize cases, identify missing documents, and compare applicant information with published property criteria. It does not approve, deny, or independently decide who deserves housing. HLB is context for affordability need—not a final property eligibility rule.

TRANSPARENT AUDIENCE LOGIC

How outreach priority works

The audience is based on program fit, not emergency need or individual eligibility.

01

Start with family-sized households

Market first includes synthetic households with exactly four or five members, matching the program's family-sized ownership units.

02

Focus on low–middle modeled income

The planning band is $75,000–$150,000 per year. It is a campaign assumption for this prototype, not an official AMI or eligibility limit.

03

Look for a realistic affordability bridge

Income must be below the modeled Household Living Budget but cover at least half of it. This avoids treating no-income households as the primary ownership-marketing audience.

04

Group households for different campaigns

Market-first households are divided into six mutually exclusive groups using child age bands and two income ranges. Each group receives a different broad channel and message; none identifies or authorizes contact with a real household.

Important limitation

The hackathon data contains synthetic household IDs and modeled geography only. The dashboard supports area and channel planning; actual outreach must use consented government or partner contact systems and comply with program policy.
