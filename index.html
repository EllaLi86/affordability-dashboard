EXPLAINABLE ELIGIBILITY

How the eligibility pre-screen works

Each rule produces a visible pass, pending, or policy-review result. There is no urgency score.

01

Income fit

Reported household income is below the modeled Household Living Budget threshold.

02

County residency

San Diego County residency is supported by accepted documentation.

03

Property ownership

The household does not already own a suitable residential property.

04

Primary residence

The household intends to occupy the purchased home as its primary residence.

05

Household and unit fit

Household size fits the family-sized homes currently planned for the program.

06

Purchase readiness

A financing, public-assistance, or homebuyer-counseling pathway has been confirmed.

07

Evidence complete

Identity, income, residency, property-ownership, and purchase-readiness evidence has been verified.

Draft policy guardrails

These prototype rules are not legal program criteria. Before launch, housing counsel must replace the modeled HLB test with the adopted income standard and confirm ownership, residency, occupancy, and household-size rules. Name, language, race, gender, disability, and other protected traits are never used.
